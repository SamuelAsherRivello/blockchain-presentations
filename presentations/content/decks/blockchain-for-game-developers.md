# Actions: Blockchain

## The player experience can have three levels

### Automatic action
The game handles it internally, off-chain.

### Delegated action
The player has authorized a bounded policy.

### Intentional action
The player directly chooses and authorizes the specific action.

<p class="catalog-guidance"><span style="color: var(--mondrian-orange)">The transaction boundary should match the player’s intent.</span></p>

# Arkade: fewer interruptions, clear control

::left::

## Routine play

Automatic actions stay inside the game loop.

The player does not sign every movement, hit, or pickup.

::right::

## Value actions

Delegated policies can cover bounded flows.

Intentional actions can ask for direct approval when the player is moving value.

<p class="catalog-guidance">Arkade’s wallet and intent model turn application policy into verifiable Bitcoin logic.</p>

# A useful approval ladder

- <span style="color: var(--mondrian-orange)">Automatic</span> — collect a coin, update a score, advance a quest
- <span style="color: var(--mondrian-orange)">Delegated</span> — renew, pay, or claim within a pre-approved limit
- <span style="color: var(--mondrian-orange)">Intentional</span> — buy armor, transfer an item, accept an offer

<p class="catalog-guidance">Arkade Intents use a quote-first flow: <strong>intent → quote → fund → fill or recover</strong>.</p>
